## In Brief

Reels are the format generating the most reach on Instagram in 2026. You do not need a videographer, an editor, or an advertising budget to benefit from them. A smartphone, 20 to 30 minutes a week, and a few reference points are enough to create business Instagram Reels that reach new customers.

---

## Why Reels Generate More Reach Than Other Formats

The 2026 numbers are clear: Reels deliver an average reach of **30.81%**, compared to 14.45% for carousels and less than 10% for static photos (source: Socialinsider). In other words, a Reel reaches on average twice as many people as a standard post, for the same audience size.

Three reasons explain this advantage:

- **The algorithm favors short video.** Instagram pushes Reels into the Explore tab and into the feeds of people who do not yet follow you.
- **55% of views come from non-followers.** It is the only format that genuinely grows your account organically.
- **200 billion Reels are watched every day** across Facebook and Instagram combined (Meta, 2026). The audience volume is there.

For a short business video, this is a direct opportunity: every Reel is a free showcase in front of people who do not know you yet.

## Formats That Work for a Small Business

You do not need original ideas. The formats that perform are simple and repeatable.

### Tradespeople and Local Retail

- **Before and after**: set up the camera, film the job site or the raw product, film the result. 15 seconds, zero commentary needed.
- **Behind the scenes**: 10 seconds of your workshop, your oven, your workbench. People love seeing how things are made.
- **Answer to a customer question**: "Do you do repairs on Saturdays?" Answer in 20 seconds on camera.

### Independent Professionals (Lawyers, Accountants, Coaches, Therapists)

- **Correcting a misconception**: "No, you do not need an accountant only at the end of the year."
- **Tip in 3 points**: quick list, subtitles, soft background music.
- **Rephrased testimonial**: describe a client's outcome (anonymized) in 30 seconds.

### B2B and Services

- **Tool or process demonstration**: show how you work, not what you sell.
- **Surprising figure**: a stat from your sector, a hook sentence, your logo at the end of the video.
- **Customer FAQ**: one question per Reel, one direct answer. You can produce 10 of them at once in an hour.

## What You Need to Get Started

The minimum equipment is what you already have.

| Item | Free or low-cost option |
|---|---|
| Camera | Recent smartphone (iPhone or Android from 2021 onward) |
| Stabilization | 15-euro tripod or leaning against a wall |
| Sound | Wired lavalier mic (20 euros) or royalty-free music |
| Editing | CapCut, Edits (Meta), or VN Video Editor, all free |
| Subtitles | Built into CapCut and Edits, automatic |

**Recommended length:**

- 7 to 15 seconds for a hook or a teaser.
- 15 to 30 seconds to present an offer or a tip.
- 30 to 60 seconds for a mini-tutorial or a demonstration.

Do not look for the "perfect" length. Look for the minimum length needed to deliver one clear message.

## Creating a Reel From Start to Finish

### 1. Filming (5 to 10 minutes)

- Film vertically, 9:16 format, minimum 1080p resolution.
- Natural light facing a window: free and effective.
- First second: start with the benefit or the tension, not with "Hi, my name is..."
- Several short takes are better than one long hesitant take.

### 2. Editing (10 minutes)

- Import into CapCut or Edits.
- Cut the silences and hesitations.
- Add automatic subtitles: 85% of videos are watched without sound.
- Choose royalty-free music from the Instagram or CapCut library.

### 3. Caption and Hashtags

- First line: restate the main benefit of the video.
- 3 to 5 targeted hashtags: one niche hashtag (e.g. #londonplumber), one sector hashtag (e.g. #craftsmanship), one general hashtag (e.g. #businesstips).
- Avoid blocks of 30 hashtags: the 2026 algorithm no longer rewards them.

## AI Voiceover for Those Who Do Not Want to Appear on Camera

Filming yourself is not mandatory. AI-generated video voiceover is a serious alternative, especially for independent professionals or business owners who prefer to stay out of the frame.

The principle is straightforward:

1. You write (or have written) a text of 50 to 100 words.
2. An AI voiceover tool generates natural narration in a few seconds.
3. You lay that voice over images, screenshots, or animated visuals.

The result is clean, professional, and requires neither a studio nor a voice actor. Today's tools handle intonation, pauses, and regional accents. For a deeper dive on this topic, the article Voix off IA : comment l'utiliser pour ses vidéos pros details the tools and concrete use cases.

## Publishing Regularly Without Spending Your Evenings on It

Consistency matters more than perfection. One Reel per week, published steadily, far outperforms three Reels in a burst followed by three weeks of silence.

**The block method:**

- Block out 90 minutes per month to film 4 to 6 short videos in one go.
- Edit them the same day, while the context is fresh.
- Schedule publication in advance: Tuesday and Thursday mornings (8am to 9am) remain strong time slots for business Instagram.

**What you can delegate or automate:**

- Generating captions and hashtags.
- Scheduling publications.
- Creating subtitles.

## FAQ

**Do you need a professional Instagram account to publish Reels?**
No, Reels are available from a personal account. But a professional or creator account gives access to detailed statistics (reach, non-follower views, completion rate), which are essential for refining your strategy. Switching is free and takes two minutes.

**How many Reels do you need to publish per week to see results?**
One Reel per week is enough to start measuring trends. Two to three per week accelerates growth, but only if quality remains consistent. The [fréquence de publication sur les réseaux sociaux](/en/blog/how-often-to-post-on-social-media) depends above all on your ability to maintain the pace over time.

**Can you reuse an Instagram Reel on other platforms?**
Yes, with a few precautions. Remove the Instagram watermark before republishing on TikTok or YouTube Shorts: the algorithms on those platforms penalize videos carrying a competitor's logo. CapCut and Edits allow you to export without a watermark.
