## LinkedIn ghostwriting: what exactly are we talking about?

**A professional writes your posts for you, in your voice, under your name, and stays invisible.**

The LinkedIn ghostwriter is not a simple copywriter. They take on your editorial identity: your convictions, your expressions, your way of structuring an idea. The post is published under your name. They disappear.

The real ratio of the job is no surprise: **20% writing, 80% extraction and strategy** (Produscale). The most complex part is not writing, it is capturing your thinking and rendering it in your tone, not theirs.

### Ghostwriter, copywriter, community manager: who does what?

| Profile | What they do | What they don't do |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghostwriter** | Writes in your voice, under your name, for your personal branding | Does not manage the company brand, does not sell a product |
| **Copywriter** | Writes to sell (sales pages, emails, landing pages) | Does not step back behind you |
| **Community manager** | Manages the brand's overall presence on social media | Does not publish under an individual's name |

### Why LinkedIn ghostwriting has exploded since 2023

LinkedIn has become the number one B2B channel. But writing 2 to 3 quality posts a week takes **45 minutes to 2 hours per post**. Multiplied by three weekly posts, that is a part-time job.

For an executive who values their time at 200 euros/hour, the opportunity cost reaches **4,800 euros/month** (Produscale). Seen from this angle, delegating the writing becomes an economic decision, not a whim.

**The profiles who turn to it:** startup founders, B2B SME executives, consultants, experienced freelancers. Common thread: real expertise, zero time for regular writing.

## How does LinkedIn ghostwriting actually work?

### Onboarding: capturing the voice (2 to 4 hours at the start)

This is the most critical phase. Rushed, it produces generic content. Done well, it produces content that sounds like you.

Good onboarding covers:

- Your background, your convictions, your verbal tics
- The topics you refuse to address
- The desired tone (didactic, direct, provocative, etc.)
- Examples of posts you admire, and ones you hate

The result: a reference **"voice guide."** It is the document that lets the ghostwriter imitate you without betraying you. Some complete it with a recording of the interview to avoid any drift over time.

### The day-to-day working rhythm

Once onboarding is complete, the rhythm settles in:

- **A 30 to 45 minute call every 2 weeks:** you deliver 4 to 6 raw ideas, anecdotes, positions
- The ghostwriter turns these raw materials into structured posts
- **Batch validation** (4 to 5 posts ahead), no word-by-word proofreading

After the first month, the no-changes validation rate reaches **70 to 80%** (Produscale). Your actual time: **1 to 2 hours per week** instead of 4 to 6 hours of writing.

## The real benefits, and the risks rarely disclosed

### What LinkedIn ghostwriting can really deliver

When the onboarding is done well and the strategy is coherent, the results after 3 to 6 months are measurable:

- **A 5x to 10x increase in monthly impressions** (Produscale)
- Inbound qualified leads via direct message
- Connection requests on the rise
- Reinforced perceived credibility, a snowball effect on the algorithm

**A concrete example:** an executive in cybersecurity multiplied their qualified leads by 4 in 6 months through a LinkedIn editorial plan run by a senior ghostwriter (Plateya).

### The risks not to underestimate

Having your LinkedIn posts written by a third party carries real risks that few providers mention spontaneously:

- **Dependence:** a freelance ghostwriter rarely handles more than **4 to 5 clients** at a time (Produscale). If they stop, your cadence collapses, and all the accumulated context disappears with them.
- **Authenticity risk:** if the onboarding is rushed, the posts sound generic. LinkedIn's audience has developed a flair for detecting content with no personal edge.
- **Cost without guarantee:** 800 to 1,500 euros/month is a significant investment for an SME. No one controls the algorithm.
- **Artificial techniques:** some providers use engagement pods or fake accounts to inflate the metrics. These practices destroy credibility in the long run.

## Human ghostwriter, agency or AI: the honest comparison

Three options exist. Each has its advantages, its limits, and its ideal profile.

| Solution | Monthly cost | Executive's time | Authenticity | Dependence risk | Ideal for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Freelance ghostwriter | 800-2,500 € | 1-2 h/week | High if onboarding is good | Strong | Executive with budget + need for strategic advice |
| Ghostwriting agency | 2,000-5,000 € | 1 h/week | Medium (standardization) | Medium | Large company, high volume |
| AI alone (not calibrated) | 20-100 € | 3-4 h/week | Low (generic content) | None | No one, disappointing results |
| AI calibrated on the voice | 50-200 € | 45 min/week | High if well configured | None | Executive who wants to keep control |
| Hybrid ghost + AI | 600-1,800 € | 1 h/week | Good | Medium | Executive with an intermediate budget |

> **Key takeaway:** the hybrid ghost + AI model cuts costs by **20 to 30%** (Produscale) while maintaining a high level of personalization. But calibrated AI alone, well configured on your voice, your values and your recurring examples, can produce comparable results for a fraction of the budget, and without any dependence on a third party.

## AI calibrated on your voice: the alternative few executives know about yet

Outsourcing LinkedIn writing no longer comes down to a choice between a human ghostwriter and an agency. AI calibrated on your voice is a third path, more accessible, more flexible, and often underestimated.

**What "calibrating AI on your voice" concretely means:**

- Giving it your 5 best posts as reference examples
- Describing your positioning, your values, your habitual expressions, your off-limits topics
- Feeding it an anecdote or a raw idea, and it generates a first draft in your tone

**What you do next:** re-read, adjust 2 to 3 sentences, validate. Count on **15 to 20 minutes per post**, versus 45 minutes to 2 hours writing on your own.

**What remains irreplaceably you:** the point of view, the lived experience, the final decision on what gets published. AI does not replace your perspective, it frees your time on the formatting.

**Result:** the consistency of a ghostwriter (2 to 3 posts/week) for an incomparable cost, and without dependence on a third party.

> **Tip:** the difference between generic AI content and calibrated AI content is the quality of the initial briefing. Spend 2 hours configuring your AI once and for all, it is the equivalent of onboarding with a ghostwriter, but you only do it once.

## How to choose between a human ghostwriter and calibrated AI?

**Choose a human ghostwriter if:**

- You have a budget of 1,000 euros+/month
- You want a long-term strategic advisory relationship
- You are in a highly technical sector where the LinkedIn writer's sector expertise really matters

**Choose calibrated AI if:**

- Your budget is limited
- You want to keep full control of your voice and your editorial line
- You can dedicate 45 min/week to validation
- You are just starting out and want to test before investing in an executive ghostwriter

**In every case, avoid:**

- Providers who promise guaranteed results
- 100% AI content with no human proofreading
- Artificial engagement pods

Delegating LinkedIn writing, whether to a human or a well-configured AI, is not a question of laziness. It is an executive's decision: focus your energy where it creates value, and entrust the formatting to a tool or a professional who respects your voice.

## FAQ

**Is LinkedIn ghostwriting ethical?**

Yes. The practice is as old as professional writing, from political speeches to executives' books to op-eds. On LinkedIn, what matters to your audience is the value of your ideas and the coherence of your voice. The fact that a professional helps you shape them betrays no one, provided the ideas remain your own.

**How long before seeing results with a LinkedIn ghostwriter?**

Count on 3 to 6 months to observe measurable effects on your impressions and your inbound leads. The first weeks serve to calibrate the voice and find the formats that resonate with your audience. Consistency is the key: two to three posts a week minimum.

**Can you outsource LinkedIn writing without losing your authenticity?**

Yes, provided the onboarding is rigorous. A well-built voice guide, precise reference examples and an active validation process ensure the posts remain your own. If you read a post and do not recognize yourself in it, that is a signal to correct immediately with your provider.

**What is the difference between having your LinkedIn posts written by a human and by calibrated AI?**

The human ghostwriter brings a strategic and relational dimension that AI does not replace: they understand your business stakes, they challenge you on your angles, they adjust the editorial strategy in real time. Calibrated AI, for its part, is available at any moment, does not cost 1,000 euros/month, and produces first drafts consistent with your voice if the briefing is done well. The two approaches are not opposed, they answer different needs and budgets.
