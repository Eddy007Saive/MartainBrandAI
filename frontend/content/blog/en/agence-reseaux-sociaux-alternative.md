## In brief

Small business owners have four options for managing their social media: an agency, a freelance or community manager, a classic scheduling tool, and an AI platform with human validation. The main decision factor is not budget alone: it is the balance between what you can spend, the time you can dedicate, and the level of results you expect. This article gives you real figures for each option and a direct recommendation based on your profile.

---

## The 4 options for managing social media

- **Social media agency**: an external team that handles strategy, creation, publishing, and reporting.
- **Freelance or community manager**: an independent contractor who manages all or part of your social presence.
- **Classic scheduling tool**: software (Buffer, Hootsuite, Metricool, Swello) that publishes what you write.
- **AI platform with human validation**: a tool that generates content calibrated to your brand, plans and publishes, with your approval before each post.

Each option has real advantages and real limitations. Here is what they look like in practice.

---

## Option 1: the social media agency

### What it costs

For a small business, budget between **1,500 and 5,000 euros per month** for serious support. Entry-level packages start around 800 to 1,500 euros for light management on one or two networks. Full setups including social ads and video production run to 8,000 euros and above.

### What an agency delivers

- Editorial strategy and content planning
- Visual creation and post copywriting
- Publishing and moderation
- Monthly reporting with metrics

### What it does not do

- Know your business without detailed, regular briefing
- React within 24 hours to news in your sector
- Guarantee results: it guarantees the service, not the performance

### Who it makes sense for

Companies with a **structured marketing budget** (at least 2,000 euros per month dedicated to social media), a need for paid campaigns, and an internal team capable of briefing the agency regularly.

### Who it is not worth the cost for

Very small businesses, self-employed professionals, and small businesses without a marketing department. If you have to explain your business to the agency at every meeting, you are paying to train someone who will never truly understand your world.

If you are thinking about [delegating your social media](/en/blog/delegate-social-media-management), an agency is worth considering seriously, but it is not the only option.

## Option 2: the freelance or community manager

### What it costs

For 2 to 4 posts per week on one or two networks, budget **300 to 800 euros per month** for a junior profile. A senior profile with content creation and strategy runs **1,000 to 1,800 euros per month**. Community manager rates vary widely depending on experience and publication volume.

### What a freelance delivers

- Consistent execution: copywriting, visuals, publishing
- Sometimes: editorial strategy for senior profiles
- Responsiveness and a direct relationship

### What they do not do

- Guarantee continuity if they are absent or leave
- Know your sector without detailed briefing from you
- Absorb a peak workload without renegotiating

### Who it makes sense for

Small businesses with a dedicated point of contact to brief the freelance, an intermediate budget (500 to 1,500 euros per month), and a need for flexibility. It is often the best value for money when the working relationship functions well.

### Risks to anticipate

- **Turnover**: if your freelance leaves, you start from scratch
- **Dependency**: all knowledge of your brand lives in their head
- **Variable quality**: without a solid brief, results follow suit

The question of doing it yourself versus delegating is especially relevant here: a freelance requires management time, even if less than an agency.

## Option 3: the classic scheduling tool

### What it costs

Buffer, Hootsuite, Metricool, Swello: between **15 and 100 euros per month** depending on the plan and the number of connected networks.

### What a scheduling tool does

- Schedule and publish at the time you choose
- Centralize multiple networks in one interface
- Provide basic analytics

### What it does not do

A classic [social media management tool](/en/blog/social-media-management-tool-small-business) creates nothing. It does not write your posts, find your ideas, or generate your visuals. It publishes what you give it, nothing more.

### Who it makes sense for

Business owners who produce their own content and simply want to automate publishing. If you enjoy writing, have ideas on a regular basis, and are looking to save time on distribution, this is the right tool.

### The main limitation

Content remains entirely your responsibility. If you are short on time or inspiration, the tool will not help: your calendar will stay empty.

## Option 4: the AI platform with human validation

### What it is

A platform that generates content calibrated to your brand (posts, visuals, planning), schedules and publishes on your networks, with your approval before each publication. You keep control without doing the creative work.

### What it costs

Between **200 and 500 euros per month** depending on the offer and the number of networks managed.

### What it delivers

- Post copywriting adapted to your tone and sector
- Visual generation consistent with your identity
- Multi-channel planning and publishing
- A dashboard to approve or adjust before publication

### What it does not do

- Replace a real brand strategy upfront: initial scoping is required
- Handle a reputation crisis or sensitive communication
- Produce highly technical content without a precise brief from you

### Who it makes sense for

Very small businesses, small businesses, and self-employed professionals who want a regular social media presence without spending time on it or paying agency prices. It is also the right option for business owners who have tried [creating content with AI](/en/blog/ai-content-creation-small-business) manually and are looking for a more structured system.

[Social media automation](/en/blog/social-media-automation) makes full sense here: you validate, you do not produce.

## How to choose based on your situation

### Tradesperson or self-employed professional, budget under 500 euros

The AI platform with human validation is the most suitable answer. You have neither the time to produce everything yourself nor the budget for a senior freelance or an agency. A regular, consistent presence for under 300 euros per month is realistic with this option.

### Small business of 5 to 20 people, budget 500 to 1,500 euros

Two viable options: a freelance community manager if you have someone internally to manage them, or an AI platform if you prefer to stay in control without an intermediary. The freelance offers more human responsiveness. The AI platform offers more consistency without dependency on a single person.

### Small business of 20 to 100 people, structured marketing budget

A social media agency becomes relevant if you have paid campaigns to run, multiple networks to manage, and an internal marketing department to handle briefing. Without those conditions, a senior freelance or an AI platform remains more cost-effective. [Social media for business owners](/en/blog/social-media-for-business-owners) does not always require a full agency: it depends on your actual objectives.

## FAQ

**Does a social media agency guarantee results?**

No. An agency guarantees a service: it publishes, creates, and reports. Results (engagement, leads, brand awareness) depend on many factors outside its direct control: your sector, your offer, the quality of the brief, the advertising budget. Be wary of agencies that promise specific figures without knowing your market.

**Can you combine multiple options?**

Yes, and it is often the best approach. For example: an AI platform for day-to-day publishing, and a freelance brought in occasionally for more complex content (videos, special campaigns). The key is not to pay twice for the same thing.

**How long does it take to see results on social media?**

Allow 3 to 6 months of consistent publishing before drawing conclusions. Social media works over time: regularity matters more than the perfection of a single post. This is precisely where automated solutions have an advantage: they maintain the pace even when you are overwhelmed.
