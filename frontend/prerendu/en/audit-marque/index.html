The #1 benefit you bring.

03 · Step

Your ideal client

To strike the right tone, the AI needs to know who it's talking to.

Who are they? Job, age, situation, level…

Beyond the product: their deeper goal.

What holds them back from saying yes.

Your client's jargon. The AI will use it to sound natural.

04 · Step

Voice & tone

The heart of the setup: how you sound.

Your brand's character in writing.

A character, a type of mentor, a vibe. Very useful to calibrate the tone.

We draw inspiration for the tone (never to copy).

05 · Step

Content pillars

Your big recurring topics: what you talk about all the time.

One per line. These are your “pillars”.

The ground where you want to be people's first thought.

Your core message, your deep conviction.

Divisive takes make the best content.

06 · Step

Red lines

What the AI must never do. Just as important as the rest.

Vocabulary that doesn't sound like you.

What we don't touch.

Important to stay credible and compliant.

Mandatory notices, regulated industry, etc.

07 · Step

Hooks & calls to action

What makes people stop scrolling, and what makes them act.

One per line. The AI draws on them to open posts.

One per line. What you ask the reader to do.

08 · Step

Editorial rules

Your “bible”: the rules the AI will follow to the letter.

Everything that overrides the rest. Structure, what's forbidden, CTA dosage…

How you build a post that works.
